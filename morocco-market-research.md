## 2. Real Scenarios — The Events Where Customers Are

### Salon du Cheval d'El Jadida (flagship event)
- **16th edition, Sept 30 – Oct 5, 2025**: ~150,000 visitors (2024: 160,000+; 200,000 expected in 2025)
- ~50 countries, 700+ riders, 1,100 horses, 600+ journalists
- Royal patronage (HM King Mohammed VI); opened by Crown Prince Moulay El Hassan
- Key competitions: Grand Prix SM le Roi (Show Jumping), Grand Prix SM le Roi (Tbourida), Barb champions cup, Arab-Barb cup, Purebred Arabian "Title Show" (new Cat. A in 2025)
- Theme 2025: "Le bien-être du cheval" — animal welfare is a marketing angle
- → **Action for Horse Haven**: exhibit at the Salon (vendor hall), or sponsor a village; this is where 150k+ of your exact target audience gathers for 6 days.

### Tbourida / Fantasia events (UNESCO Intangible Heritage since 2021)
- ~1,000 troupes nationally; 330+ in national championship; 5,900 dedicated horses
- 2025 Throne Day festival, Rabat: **500 riders, 50+ sorbas**, 4 days, full houses
- Sidi Rahal festival (13th ed, 2025): 42 sorbas, **800+ horses/riders, ~30,000 visitors**
- Ain Chkef/Moulay Yacoub festival: 42 sorbas, 600 horses
- Hassan II Tbourida Trophy (Rabat, June): 24 elite sorbas (18 senior + 6 junior)
- → **Action**: Tbourida sorbas need: saddles (selles), rifle holsters, traditional tack, horse blankets, grooming. Partner with mokaddems (team leaders) for bulk orders.

### Horse racing (7 hippodromes)
- 2,400–3,000 races/year; 2,852 horses raced in 2016 (+31% vs 2015); 12 starters/race avg
- → Racing owners/trainers buy: stable rugs, bandages, feed supplements, grooming products.

### Riding schools & clubs (150+ centers)
- Real prices (MesLoisirs.ma 2026):
  - Lesson: 200–500 MAD/hour
  - Monthly membership: 2,000–4,000 MAD
  - Private clubs: Royal Club Equestre Marrakech, Casa Anfa (est. 1912), Bouskoura, Mohammedia, Mamora, Rabat/Temara, Fès, Meknès, Agadir, Essaouira (Diabat), Taroudant, Ifrane
- Polo clubs: Marrakech (Royal Polo Club, Jnan Amar), Rabat — initiation 500–1,500 MAD/session, resorts with 40-horse stables
- → **Action**: B2B2C — supply clubs with tack inventory, rider gear (helmets, boots, chaps), stable equipment. Clubs = recurring volume buyers.

### Equestrian tourism (new and growing)
- Ifrane: **181 km of marked trails** inaugurated May 2026 (Fès-Meknès region + French Centre-Val de Loire cooperation, 1.4M MAD invested) — first national equestrian tourism itinerary
- Target: 230 km with loops; guides being trained with FFE/FITE
- Tourism prices: 1h ride 150–600 MAD; multi-day Atlas treks 2,500–20,000 MAD
- → **Action**: sell trail/hiking gear (fly sheets, travel boots, pack saddles), plus tourism operators as B2B clients.

## 4. Import Costs — Real Scenario Math (Customs)

### How Moroccan import taxes work (douane.gov.ma, 2026 rules)
```
Valeur CIF = FOB + Fret + Assurance
Droit d'Importation (DI) = CIF × rate (0–30%)
TPF (Parafiscale) = CIF × 0.25%
Base TVA = CIF + DI + TPF
TVA = Base × 20% (standard)
Total = DI + TPF + TVA
```

### Duty rates by category (PLF 2026, circulaire ADII 6702/210)
| Category | DI rate |
|----------|---------|
| Raw materials | 0% |
| Industrial equipment | 2.5% |
| Semi-finished | 10% |
| Finished consumer goods | **25%** (tack, clothing, saddles likely here) |
| Heavily protected | 30% (textiles may apply) |

- **KEY LEVER**: Products of **EU origin under EUR.1 certificate = 0% duty** (Morocco-EU Association Agreement 2000). Most equestrian brands are European → import from France/Germany/Spain/Italy with EUR.1 to zero out DI.
- TVA import is recoverable if you're VAT-registered (mandatory >2M MAD turnover for commercial activity).
- Fret example: 20ft container China→Casablanca: $2,000–6,000; air freight = 10–20% of FOB.

### Worked example — importing €5,000 (≈53,500 MAD) of tack from France (with EUR.1)
```
FOB:                53,500 MAD
Fret + insurance:    4,500 MAD
CIF:                58,000 MAD
DI (0% with EUR.1):      0 MAD
TPF (0.25%):           145 MAD
Base TVA:           58,145 MAD
TVA (20%):          11,629 MAD
Total to customs:   11,774 MAD  (~17% of landed cost 69,774 MAD)
```
Without EUR.1 (e.g., China origin): DI 25% = 14,500 MAD → base TVA = 72,645 → TVA 20% = 14,529 → total ≈ 29,174 MAD (~50% of CIF). **Always buy from EU suppliers.**

| Player | Type | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| **Decathlon.ma** | Mass retailer | 16 equestrian products listed; saddle pads 89–349 MAD; saddles on sale 1,999–3,999 MAD; entry-level only, no pro gear |
| **Maroc Marechalerie SARL (marechalpro.com)** | Specialist B2B | Casablanca; farrier tools, horseshoes, nails, resins + some tack (numnahs, lunge equipment); WooCommerce shop with cart — the closest existing specialist |
| **Cavalor Maroc (cavalormaroc.com)** | Feed brand distributor | Belgian feed brand sold in Morocco, e.g., Juniorix 20kg = 285 MAD — shows premium feed market exists |
| **Fibersoil (Casablanca)** | Equestrian surfaces | Arena sand, footing for riding schools (B2B infra, not retail) |
| Facebook/Instagram resellers | Informal | Many small sellers of tack/feed via DMs + COD — the main current channel; no trust, no warranty |
| **Online boutiques (equi-clic.com, cheval-shop.com, equiphorse.com)** | EU competitors | Ship to Morocco only via private freight; slow, expensive, no local support |

### Gap analysis → Horse Haven positioning
1. **No dedicated Moroccan e-commerce specialist for equestrian gear** exists (only farrier tools + feed)
2. Decathlon covers entry-level only; pros buy abroad or via informal resellers
3. B2B demand (150 clubs, 1,000 sorbas, 7 hippodromes) is unserved online
4. EU origin + EUR.1 = duty-free import = competitive pricing vs informal resellers
5. Equestrian is a **low-density niche** in the fastest-growing e-commerce market in Africa

## 7. Actionable Strategy (based on the data)

### Phase 1 — Launch (Months 1–3)
- **Catalog focus**: saddle pads, bridles, boots/protections, grooming, fly sheets (Morocco = hot climate; heavy winter rugs are dead stock), rider apparel, helmets
- **Payments**: CMI card + **COD** + WhatsApp ordering (dominant behavior)
- **Mobile-first store**, French + Arabic + English
- **Sales channels**: Instagram/TikTok shops (62% of population uses social commerce) + store website

### Phase 2 — B2B (Months 3–6)
- Target: 150+ riding clubs, 1,000+ sorbas, 7 hippodrome trainers
- Bulk/contract offers for clubs (tack, blankets, grooming, feed supplements)
- Partner with **Salon du Cheval d'El Jadida** (150k visitors, Oct) — exhibit or run a pop-up
- Partner with **Cavalor/feed brands** already importing to Morocco (cross-sell)

### Phase 3 — Tourism & Premium (Months 6–12)
- Trail gear for the new Ifrane itinerary (181 km) and Essaouira beach operations
- Premium saddles/brands for polo clubs (Marrakech, Rabat) and pro riders

### Key numbers for the business plan
- TAM: 170,000 horses × avg. 1,000 MAD/year equipment spend = **170M MAD/year**
- SAM: 20,000 riders + 150 clubs + 1,000 sorbas + racing owners
- Market is growing: e-comm +30%/yr, equine GDP + faster than national GDP, births +30%/decade

### Risks (real)
- 25–30% duty if sourcing outside EU → **source EU only (EUR.1 = 0% duty)**
- COD = cash-flow friction and returns risk → cap COD on big items, use CMI cards (47% adoption)
- Import delays at Casablanca/Tanger Med ports → keep stock buffer, use a licensed customs broker
- Seasonality: sales peak Oct (Salon) & around moussems; plan promotions accordingly
- Price sensitivity: average basket 380 MAD → entry price points + premium tier for pros
