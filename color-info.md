# Horse Haven Logo - Color Analysis

## Brand Information
- **Brand Name**: HORSE HAVEN
- **Tagline**: "le refuge pour l'excellence équestre"
- **Logo Elements**: Horse head, Italian flag brush stroke, green star, lotus flower, circular frame

## Primary Colors

### Background Colors
| Color | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|-------|-----|-----|-------|
| Deep Blue | `#2B5A83` | rgb(43, 90, 131) | Main background |
| Light Blue | `#B8CCE4` | rgb(184, 204, 228) | Logo circle fill |

### Text & Logo Colors
| Color | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|-------|-----|-----|-------|
| Dark Navy | `#1A2744` | rgb(26, 39, 68) | Horse illustration, subtitle text |
| Champagne Gold | `#D4C5A9` | rgb(212, 197, 169) | Main title "HORSE HAVEN", decorative lines |

### Accent Colors
| Color | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|-------|-----|-----|-------|
| Italian Green | `#006B3F` | rgb(0, 107, 63) | Flag brush stroke, star |
| Italian Red | `#CC3333` | rgb(204, 51, 51) | Flag brush stroke |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | rgb(255, 255, 255) | Flag brush stroke, highlights |

### Frame & Effects
| Color | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|-------|-----|-----|-------|
| Silver/Chrome | `#C0C0C0` | rgb(192, 192, 192) | Circular frame border |
| Warm Glow | `#FFE4B5` | rgb(255, 228, 181) | Light effects on frame |

## Design Notes
- The logo has an elegant, premium equestrian aesthetic
- Italian flag colors suggest Italian heritage or connection
- Blue tones convey trust, professionalism, and calmness
- Gold accents add luxury and sophistication
- Lotus flower adds a zen/wellness element
