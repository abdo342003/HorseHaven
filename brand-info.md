## Summary

Horse Haven is a French online equestrian store offering a curated selection of:
- Horse equipment (rugs, bridles, boots)
- Horse materials (saddles, halters, blankets, care products)
- Rider & stable items (clothing, protection)

The brand emphasizes quality, security, fast delivery, and excellent customer service, targeting both amateur and professional riders.
